# Trump and Play Restrictions with AI

This milestone adds five supported cards to the default evolution deck. It uses the existing four-zone rule field and Game Rules review. The starting field still has exactly **Follow Suit** and **High Card Wins**. The hidden **No Trump** baseline is replaced only when a trump card is revealed. The defaults for hand size, hand limit, and reveal interval still come from `Config`.

## Card semantics

| Card | Category | Effect and replacement | Draw eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spades Are Trump | Trick | Spades are trump each hand. Played trump is the only winning suit; otherwise the lead suit is eligible. Active high/low rank and earlier-play tie rules compare eligible cards. Replaces No Trump or Pitch the Trump. | Initially eligible |
| Pitch the Trump | Trick | The first card led each hand sets trump for that hand, including the first trick. The existing leader still opens. Replaces No Trump or Spades Are Trump. | Initially eligible |
| Follow Suit or Trump | Play | On a nontrump lead, a player holding the lead suit may follow it or play trump. If void, any card is legal. On a trump lead, follow trump if able. Replaces the active play restriction; Follow Suit's independent lead-suit eligibility remains subordinate to played trump. | Requires active Spades Are Trump **or** Pitch the Trump |
| Break Spades | Play | Until a spade is played off suit, spades cannot be led unless only spades remain. Requires fixed Spades Are Trump; Pitch deactivates it. | Requires active Spades Are Trump |
| Break Hearts | Play | Until a heart is played off suit, hearts cannot be led unless only hearts remain. | Initially eligible |

Both break cards can coexist. If their combined unbroken lead bans leave no legal lead, any card may be led. This is shown in accessible full rule text. Broken-suit flags are public facts recorded on accepted off-suit plays even when no break card is active. They reset on each new deal. Fixed spades trump is selected at hand start; pitched trump is unset until the first lead and resets before the next hand. Reveals occur after a completed hand, so new mechanics first affect the next deal. Ineligible cards stay in the draw, and no rule is revealed when none is eligible or after the final hand.

The catalog is version **3**. Version 1/2 match records and incompatible rule shuffle orders fail explicit validation instead of inheriting changed semantics. Low Card Wins and No Follow Required remain supported compatibility fixtures outside the default deck. No bidding, kitty, passing, teams, penalty score, rank variant, or new victory system is added.

## Engine, projections, and presentation

`ResolveRules` is still the sole interpreter of effective mechanics, active field, and retained replacement history. It supports an any-of prerequisite group for Follow Suit or Trump. A later explicit trump replacement controls trump; a previously superseded evolution card does not revive if the later card is removed. A dependent Break Spades card becomes inactive when Pitch replaces fixed spades. Current rules and the field exclude inactive effects; Changes keeps the history and explains the missing prerequisite.

`legalCardsWithRules` separates lead bans and following restrictions from `WinningPlay`, which compares eligibility and rank. `TrumpForTrick` supplies fixed or pitched trump, including a hypothetical opening lead for bot evaluation. Engine actions, `LegalCards`, local legal highlighting, the heuristic, and rollout continuations use these shared typed helpers. Authoritative rejected plays and local feedback name the applicable rule. Public projections include selected trump and broken flags, but neither private opponent cards nor future rule order. Accepted card-play events carry public trump selection and break transitions; the field, Game Rules, and status cue update from the same projection. Reconnect gets current facts without replaying old reveal events. The canvas and animation path are unchanged.

## AI and sampling

The heuristic uses shared legal cards and `WinningPlay`. It values a trump winner without assuming trump permits an otherwise illegal play, conserves trump when a cheaper card wins, and considers suit length when choosing a Pitch opening lead. Rollouts still use `domain.Step` for every simulated play, the same sampled world for each candidate in a comparison round, and isolated copied state per continuation. They retain the existing hand-end score-margin objective and default bounds: **8 worlds per candidate, 80 ms, 1,000 steps**. These bounds are not increased by this milestone.

The sampler reconstructs selected/unset trump, broken flags, public rule history and scores, visible trick, captured cards, and this bot's private hand. It never accepts a live opponent hand. Each observed play stores its contemporaneous play restriction and trump suit so later replacements cannot reinterpret it. Strict following makes an off-suit play prove a void in the lead suit. Under Follow Suit or Trump, an off-suit **trump** does not prove a void, while an off-suit **nontrump** does. Failure to follow a trump lead proves a void in trump. The constrained sampler excludes a proven void suit from that player's remaining hand; a missed inference is preferable to a false constraint. Test-only fully specified worlds compare reconstructed public facts and continuing engine steps with the original state. When the 80 ms wall budget expires after completed comparison rounds, the bot uses those rounds; an incomplete round is discarded. Room cancellation and zero completed rounds still use the A1 fallback.

Updated ten-hand engine and HTTP/WebSocket fixtures cover cadence, replay, scoring, dealer/turn order, reconnect, private-hand isolation and bounded full games with the new default deck.
